\subsection{Setup}
We evaluate on the MotionBenchMaker (MBM) benchmark using UR5, Panda, and Baxter
($N{=}2858$ problems). All methods use identical joint-space planning problems, robot
models, joint limits, and time budgets, with one attempt per problem; the exception is
PRM, which receives cuRobo's stock five-attempt budget, as its dominant failure mode is
returning no seed at all and its roadmap draw is stochastic. We vary only the seed
supplied to cuRobo TrajOpt: linear interpolation (LERP), cuRobo's PRM fallback, or a
CPU-generated VAMP-RRTC path transferred through CUDA managed memory. Success requires a
dynamically feasible, time-parameterized trajectory.~\cref{fig:exp1_headline} reports
success rate, planning time, and joint-space path length, while~\cref{fig:fig_phase_breakdown}
decomposes runtime by phase.

VAMP-RRTC seeding achieves a pooled success rate of 74.1\%, compared with 67.3\% for LERP
and 65.9\% for PRM, with mean planning times of 305, 292, and 621\,ms, respectively. The
largest gains occur for UR5 (27.0\% to 38.2\%) and Panda (83.3\% to 98.9\%); Baxter improves
marginally from 78.6\% to 79.3\%, as its remaining failures are predominantly dynamic rather
than geometric. Successful VAMP-seeded trajectories are 1--2\% longer than LERP trajectories
(4.56 vs.\ 4.50\,rad), although this comparison is conservative because each mean is computed
over the problems successfully solved by that method.

VAMP-RRTC requires 14.7\,ms for seeding, compared with 266.4\,ms for PRM across its five
draws. Transferring the CPU-generated seed to the GPU costs only 0.41\,ms, or 0.13\% of total
planning time. TrajOpt runtime is effectively unchanged relative to LERP (289.4 vs.\
291.8\,ms), indicating that the improved success rate results from better initialization
rather than additional optimization. Thus, managed memory enables a high-quality CPU seeder
with negligible transfer overhead, outperforming cuRobo's GPU PRM seed in success rate at
roughly half its total planning time, though PRM's budget spans five attempts.
